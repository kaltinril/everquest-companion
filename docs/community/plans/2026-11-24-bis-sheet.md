Why it changed: Malkil's ranger board (RNG, MAG, WIZ; DEX, Attack, Haste, Ranged ratio in S; 15

### The rules (sources in `agent/MEASURED.md`, "BiS sheet research")

- **No input from `roleWeights.ts`.** Defaults, units and weights are all new.
- **Lane weight = step^(3 - lane index)**, C = 1. Step 4 by default (S 64, A 16, B 4, C 1, D 1/4,
  F 1/16); 2 and 25 ("S always wins") selectable. One S stat outweighs four of the same size in C.
- **Derived stats.** HP includes STA at `level x F / 3000` HP a point, F = the highest HP factor
  among the picked classes (at 60: WAR 300, PAL/SHD/BER 260, RNG 200, MNK/BRD/ROG/BST 180,
  CLR/DRU/SHM 150, NEC/WIZ/MAG/ENC 120). Mana includes INT and WIS at `0.1875 x level` mana a point
  (the stat between 100 and 200), each only for picked classes that cast from it (WIS: CLR DRU SHM
  PAL RNG BST; INT: NEC WIZ MAG ENC SHD BRD). Both stats are counted when both kinds are picked:
  the Legends multiclass rule is not published.
- **Resists** count as the average of fire, cold, magic, disease and poison; "Split resists" gives
  the five back. SV Void is its own stat. SV Corruption is gone: no item states it.
- **Units** = the median positive derived value over in-era endgame rows (drop level over 50, or a
  Legends revamp era tag), all rows when fewer than 15. A unit is "what a good endgame item
  carries", so S DEX means DEX the way items give it.
- **Defaults** from class guides (Almar's classic guides, plus the Legends rules: archery on DEX,
  STR melee only): per class a tier per stat; several classes take the best tier any of them gives;
  a stat no picked class can use is Off. Presets: My classes, Tank, Melee, Ranged, Caster, Healer.
- **Item budget**: no public formula (research), and our fit finds none (items of one drop level
  vary about 4x, p10 0.37 to p90 1.5 of the fitted budget). Each item shows its total (sum of value
  / unit over the derived stats) against the median of its slot and level band (1-20, 21-35, 36-50,
  51+, Legends revamp).
- **Typical amounts by band** (non-weapon gear, median of stated positive values, 2026-11-24):

  | Band | Attribute | HP | Mana | AC | One resist | Haste |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | lvl 36-50 | 5 | 20 | 20 | 9 | 5 | 22% |
  | lvl 51+ | 6 | 40 | 35 | 14 | 5 | 36% |
  | Legends revamp | 5 | 25 | 25 | 10 | 5 | 41% |

- Kept from version 1: the 23 cells, ratios only where the weapon swings and never in ANY, the
  dual-wield list, Lore placed once, a two-hander closing SECONDARY, haste counted once per loadout,
  caps (now on the derived stats; the Legends attribute cap is 510), owned-only, the Off lane.

### What the BEST formula is as a tier list (measured 2026-11-24)

Importance of a stat = its BEST weight x the median value items state for it (6,975 gear rows,
all eras, all classes; weapon ratios split by the weapon's skill). That is "how much BEST pays for
a typical piece of this stat". Bucketed with the geometric mean of the stated stats on C:

| Tier | Stats (items that state it) |
| --- | --- |
| S | Haste (66), HP regen (3), ATK (2), AC (4,662), HP (1,405), Mana regen (2) |
| A | none |
| B | none |
| C | 2H ratio (444), MP (1,313), STR (1,720), Backstab (244), Ranged ratio (95), STA (1,248), DEX (1,057), INT (1,193) |
| D | 1H ratio (1,098), WIS (1,338), End regen (2), SV Fire, Cold, Magic, Disease, Poison (~900-1,300 each), SV Void (5) |
| F | AGI (913), CHA (747), DMG bonus (57) |
| Off | END (67; BEST does not read it); SV Corruption, Chromatic, Prismatic, All (no item states one) |

What that shows, and why the owner's "made up" is fair:

- The formula is lumpy: it jumps from S to C. A tier list built from it will look the same.
- Haste (median 36% x 2 = 72) is the most valuable stat per item by a wide margin. Only the
  "haste worn" rule keeps it from topping every list.
- HP regen, ATK, mana regen and end regen are stated as stats on 2 or 3 items each. Their weights
  matter almost nowhere. (Worth checking in phase 1: regen may live in worn effects instead.)
- 1H and 2H ratios land in different tiers only because a two-hander's ratio is bigger. With
  Malkil's three ratio stats each gets its own tier.
- The built default (P1, `defaultBoard`) differs from this table in one way: a resist no item
  states (SV Corruption) takes the other resists' unit and lane rather than Off, so a board stays
  consistent when SV All lands on it. SV All, Chromatic and Prismatic are not chips (they count
  toward every resist chip).

## Design

### The stats on the board

Each is a chip. Excluded by Malkil: Race, Class, Size, Weight, Skill, Lore Equipped, No Trade,
Placeable, Exaltations. Not stats (per-item facts): charges, cooldown, cast time, required level.

| Group | Chips | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Defense | AC, HP, STA, AGI | HP and STA become two chips (today one effective-HP term) |
| Attributes | STR, DEX, WIS, INT, CHA | |
| Pools and regen | MP, END, HP regen, Mana regen, End regen | |
| Offense | ATK, Haste, DMG bonus, Backstab | DMG bonus gets its own chip (today folded into the ratio) |
| Weapons | 1H ratio, 2H ratio, Ranged ratio | Malkil: three stats. 1H/2H by the weapon's skill (`gearHandedness`), ranged by `isRangedWeapon` |
| Resists | SV Fire, Cold, Magic, Disease, Poison, Void, Corruption | one chip each; SV All, Chromatic, Prismatic count toward every resist chip |
| Later (question 5) | Fire/Cold/Poison/Magic/Bane DMG, weapon Range, Skill mods, Proc, Worn effect, Focus effect, Click effect | effects score by presence only: the corpus states no size for them |

A chip that no candidate item states (after class, era and level) is drawn grey, so nobody tiers a
stat that changes nothing.

### Tiers to numbers

- Tier weights: S 8, A 5, B 3, C 2, D 1, F 0.5, Off 0. One constant table; tuned with the owner
  (question 2).
- Each stat has a UNIT: the median value items state for it, measured from the gear index at load
  (not hard-coded). So "S" means the same for STR (unit 5) as for HP (unit 25) or haste (36%).
- Item score = sum over its stats of tier weight x stated value / unit. A stated negative is a
  penalty (law 1). Absent stat = nothing.
- Order within a lane does not change the score (question 9).
- The upgrade slider applies: the score reads the scaled vector, as BEST does today.
- Rules that stay rules, not weights:
  - Weapon ratios count only in PRIMARY, SECONDARY, RANGE. Never ANY1, ANY2 (Malkil), never EAR
    and the like (existing rule).
  - A 1H ratio counts in SECONDARY only when a picked class can dual wield (question 4).
  - Haste does not stack: in the sheet, the loadout counts its single best haste once. In the BEST
    column (one item, no loadout) the existing worn-haste rule and Ignore haste chip stay.
  - Class gate: decides the DEFAULT tier of a dead stat (a warrior's INT starts in Off). The player
    may still move it: the point of the feature is that the player decides.

### The default tier list is our formula

- Importance = BEST weight x unit (the table above), for the classes picked; bucketed as measured.
- Default follows the class picks until the player edits the board; after that the board is
  theirs. "Reset to default" puts it back.
- The same mapping turns each Recommended-tab focus into a preset: Balanced (= BEST), Tank,
  Healer, 1H DPS, 2H DPS, Dual wield, Ranged, DD, DOT.
- BEST column then reads the active tier list. Header hover: "Scored by your tier list (Best in
  slot tab)".
- Calibration, before BEST switches: per slot, old BEST top 10 against tier BEST top 10 (overlap
  and rank correlation), over the corpus at the default picks and at the owner's trio. Numbers go
  in this file; the owner decides whether the switch stands (question 8). It will not be exact:
  tiers round the weights.
- **Measured 2026-11-24** (`tests/tierCalibration.test.mts`, 6,975 rows, 18 slots, slot-free on
  both sides as the Gear table scores; floors pinned a little under these):

  | Classes | Top-10 overlap, mean | Worst slot | Spearman, mean | Worst slot |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | none picked | 8.7 / 10 | 6 (BACK) | 0.991 | 0.958 (WAIST) |
  | WAR / MNK / SHM | 8.8 / 10 | 5 (BACK) | 0.991 | 0.955 (WAIST) |

  The default board keeps nearly the whole ranking; most slots lose one or two of their top ten.
- The default boards as built (units: AC 8, HP 25, STA/STR/DEX/AGI/INT 5, WIS/CHA 4, MANA 25,
  HP regen 6, mana regen 2, ATK 40, haste 36, DMG bonus 15, backstab 6, ratios 1H 0.27 / 2H 0.48 /
  ranged 0.33, resists 5, SV Void 4):

  | Lane | No class picked | WAR / MNK / SHM |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | S | AC, HP, HP regen, Mana regen, ATK, Haste | the same |
  | A | none | none |
  | B | 2H ratio | none |
  | C | STA, STR, DEX, INT, MANA, Backstab, Ranged ratio | STA, STR, DEX, MANA, 2H ratio, Ranged ratio |
  | D | WIS, End regen, 1H ratio, every resist | the same |
  | F | AGI, CHA, DMG bonus | AGI, DMG bonus |
  | Off | END | INT, CHA, END, Backstab |

- Presets are the same mapping over the other eight focuses (`PRESET_ROLES`); the dps ones at the
  survivability default 0.3. They inherit the role model's lumps (2H DPS puts all three ratios in
  S, Ranged puts STR and ATK in Off).

### Crossing a stat off (owner)

- Phase 3: an **Off** lane under F, grey, chips struck through, labelled "Doesn't count (capped)".
  Drag a chip there, or click the chip and pick Off. Weight 0, in the sheet and in BEST.
- "Not as much" is a lower lane: drag STR from S to D.
- Phase 4, caps: a chip can carry a cap number. The sheet totals the loadout and counts a stat only
  up to its cap; above the cap a point is worth nothing. The current total comes from the worn
  items in the inventory dump (`/outputfile inventory`), or from the sheet's own loadout. Haste is
  the built-in case. Caps are what the player types: the repo has no sourced cap data (question 6).
  A chip whose worn total reaches its cap shows "capped" and can move itself to Off (offered, not
  automatic).

### The sheet

- Cells: the 23 `PLAN_SLOTS` (EAR and EAR 2, WRIST and WRIST 2, FINGER and FINGER 2, AMMO, ANY 1,
  ANY 2), character-sheet order.
- Candidates: equippable rows a picked class can use; Current era chip; optional "owned only"
  (bags, bank, worn from the dump); optional max level (`reqLevel`, question 11). A row whose class
  list the page did not state: question 10.
- Two-handers: a 2H in PRIMARY closes SECONDARY. The sheet compares best 2H against best 1H +
  best SECONDARY and shows the loser as a runner-up.
- Lore: an item flagged Lore Equipped or Lore Item is placed at most once across all cells,
  ANY cells included. Any other item may fill two cells (two of the same ring). In owned-only mode
  an item fills at most as many cells as copies owned.
- ANY 1, ANY 2: any equippable item, scored with no ratio terms (a stat stick there).
- Solver (as built, `bisSearch.ts`): score every admitted row once per cell; keep the top 12 per
  cell; choose exactly. The lore and copy limits make it an assignment of cells to item copies,
  solved by the Hungarian method; the two-hander and one-haste rules are enumerated around it (with
  or without a two-hander; which placed item supplies the haste, or none). Branch and bound, the
  plan's first idea, ran out a 400k-node budget on the corpus: the ANY cells' best rows are the
  other cells' lore picks, so every bound counted them twice. Measured 2026-11-24 over 6,975 rows:
  112 ms with no class picked, 72 ms for WAR/MNK/SHM. Pinned against brute force on 400 random
  small sheets (`tests/bisSearch.test.mts`).
- Not modelled: a "Lore Group" (2 pages) makes its members exclude each other in the game; each is
  only limited to itself here. GearRow states no required level, so the level cap is the caller's
  `admit` predicate (P3 decides what it reads).
- Each cell shows: icon and item, score, the two or three stats that earned it, owned or worn mark,
  where it drops (zone and mob, as the Gear table), the change against the worn item, and an
  expander with the next five.
- Under the cells: loadout totals per stat against worn totals.

### Where it lives and how it looks

- A new gear-area tab, **Best in slot**, between Gear and Recommended (view id `bis`, question 12).
- Top row: classes (the Gear tab's class picks, shared, question 7), preset menu, Reset, Current
  era, Owned only, upgrade slider.
- The board: the reference image's look in CSS (colours above), plus the Off lane. Chips in the
  app's own spelling (STR, SV FIRE, 1H RATIO). Drag between lanes; or click a chip for a small menu
  S A B C D F Off (keyboard, and no drag-only control).
- Below or beside: the sheet.
- Stored per machine: `eq.gear.tiers` (board, caps, preset it came from). Per character later.

## Suggestions (Malkil asked for them)

1. Presets from the Recommended tab's nine focuses, so nobody starts from a blank board.
2. Owned-only mode: "best from what I already have", beside "best overall".
3. Per cell, the change against what is worn now, and why (the stats that earned it), like the
   Void motes tab's reasoning lines.
4. Runners-up per cell, so a player who cannot get the top item sees the next.
5. Haste as a loadout stat (one source counts) rather than a per-item number.
6. Grey chips for stats nothing in the candidate set states.
7. A board as a short text string to paste in Discord or a video description, and back.
8. A max level, so a level-30 sees what they can wear now.
9. One board drives BEST, the sheet, and later the Recommended tab and Void motes, so the app never
   ranks one item two ways (the role model's own law).
